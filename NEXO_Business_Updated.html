FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

How do Business QR Scanner & B2B Request work?

Tap the center QR Scanner button in the bottom navigation to scan or paste an Agent/Merchant QR (`NEXO:AGENT:...` or `NEXO:MERCHANT:...`) for instant transfers.

How do Agent & Merchant services differ?

Agents access Cash In, B2B Transfer, Remittance, Micro Finance & Float Tools; Merchants access Agent Cash Out, NEXO to Card, Send Money, Merchant Pay & Cancel Txn reversal.
