FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

How do Linked Wallets & Redeem work?

Link multiple NEXO Pay accounts in Top Up → Linked Wallets using each account's 11-digit number and 6-digit NEXO Pay PIN. Then select any linked wallet, enter an amount, and confirm via the slide-up PIN pad.

How do Card Freeze & Custom Limits work?

Toggle Freeze anytime to block spending, switch Card Metal finishes, enable PIN protection for Show Info, and customize your Single Transaction Limit (৳1,000 – ৳1,00,000) in Card Controls.
